About RN Real Estate

Where healthcare values meet real estate expertise

Our Story

RN Real Estate was founded on a simple belief: nurses make the best realtors. Detail-oriented, empathetic, and calm under pressure — these aren't just healthcare traits, they're exactly what you need when navigating one of the biggest financial decisions of your life.

Based in Greenwood, Indiana, we serve buyers, sellers, and builders across all of Central Indiana. Whether you're a first-time buyer or seasoned investor, our team brings the same thoroughness to your transaction as we brought to patient care.

We cater to the real estate needs of all of Central Indiana

Comprehensive services for buyers, sellers, individuals planning to build, and commercial real estate. Whatever your real estate goal, we're here every step of the way.
